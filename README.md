# JavaScript 2 - Extra Lessons

Optional extra lessons and short reads for students taking JavaScript 2 at Noroff.

Some of these go over the same ground as the regular Moodle lessons from a different angle. Others go a little further than the course does, or give you a quick way into a tool you'll meet along the way.

> [!NOTE]
> These lessons are extra material for your own benefit. They don't replace the regular lessons, and they are not part of what you are assessed on. Where an extra lesson and the course material seem to disagree, go with the course material, and let your teacher know.
